## What

Two related directives from Ken during the 1 Sept team meeting, in the context of Tai Sin's
outstanding pre-go-live issues (12 items at the time, most since resolved — see
[[2026-09-04_ken_golive-date-confirmed-14sep]]):

1. **Regular customer updates, ideally every ~4 hours** while issues are being actively worked,
   rather than going quiet for a week and letting the customer assume nothing is happening. Keep a
   running list of outstanding vs. resolved issues so the customer (and the team) can see progress
   ("today we fixed 5 of 10", "9 of 10 fixed, one left") rather than everyone waiting on everyone
   else.
2. **Plan for support handover.** Ken does not want Dyah to be the permanent single point of
   contact for every Tai Sin support/troubleshooting request. He wants a plan for training a junior
   (e.g. an intern) to do first-level support and troubleshooting — using a searchable knowledge
   base of past issues/fixes — before anything escalates to Dyah. Dyah mentioned she already logs
   most of her TS app work and troubleshooting into Track (Plane); Ken's caveat: that's only useful
   if the future support person can actually find the right entry by keyword, and that indexing
   problem needs solving deliberately (a filter-by-product + keyword search that surfaces likely
   matches), not solved by "ask AI every time" — AI-assisted search is fine to get there, but the
   end state for a new support person should be a good index, not a dependency on prompting AI well.

## Why

Ken, in the 1 Sept meeting: motivated by wanting predictable, visible progress to customers instead
of silence, and by not wanting himself or senior devs to remain bottlenecked on support long-term as
the team takes on more apps/customers.

## Impact

- This cadence expectation is general (Ken repeated the sentiment about visibility/dashboards in
  the 9 Sept and 11 Sept meetings re: the team dashboard project) — worth a durable memory entry if
  it recurs again, but captured here against its concrete Tai Sin instance for now.
- Actionable: draft a support-handover/knowledge-base plan for Tai Sin (proposed as a Plane issue
  separately — see session report, not duplicated here).
